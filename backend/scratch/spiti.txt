Day 06
Explore Key, Komic, Langza, and Hikkim
in a day
Exploring the Wonders of Spiti Valley
Visit Key Monastery, the largest Tibetan Buddhist
monastery in Spiti, known for its murals, manuscripts, and
spiritual aura.
Explore Komic, the world’s highest motorable village (15,050
ft), and its ancient Komic Gompa.
Witness the majestic Buddha Statue at Langza (14,300 ft)
and discover fossils from an ancient seabed.
Send a postcard from Hikkim, home to the world’s highest
post office (14,400 ft).
Breakfast,Dinner Included 
Stay: Homestay in Kaza

Spiti Valley to Chandra Taal – A Journey to the Moon Lake
Visit Kibber, a stunning high-altitude village known for its
barren beauty and monasteries.
Cross the Chicham Bridge, the highest bridge in Asia, offering
breathtaking views.
Travel to Chandra Taal, the mesmerizing "Moon Lake" on the
Samudra Tapu plateau.
Overnight stay at Chandra Taal Camp.
   Note:
 Chandra Taal Lake usually opens by late Mid June. If
inaccessible, we stay in Kaza and reroute via Kalpa and Shimla.
