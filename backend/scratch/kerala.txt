Day 01
Ahmedabad to Cochin Train Journey
Kickstart your Kerala adventure with a comfortable
overnight train journey from Ahmedabad to Kochi. Sit back,
enjoy the scenic route, make new friends, and gear up for
the beautiful experiences that await in God's Own Country.
Upon arrival, guests are requested to make their own
arrangements for transportation from the railway station to
the hotel, as transfers on this day are not included in the
package.
Day 02
Arrival in Cochin
Upon arriving at Ernakulam Junction around 9:00 PM,
proceed to your hotel near the railway station and settle
in after your long train journey. 
Take the evening to relax and recharge, as no sightseeing
is scheduled today, ensuring everyone is well-rested for
the exciting Kerala adventure ahead.
Stay: Hotel in Cochin 
Day 03
 
Breakfast & Dinner Included 
Stay: Hotel in Munnar 
Scenic Drive to Munnar
Your unforgettable Kerala journey begins with a warm
pick-up from your respective Hotel.
Enjoy a breathtaking drive to Munnar through lush green
hills and misty landscapes. On the way, witness the
beauty of the stunning Valara & Cheeyappara Waterfalls
and explore aromatic Spice Plantations, where Kerala’s
famous spices are grown.
Arrive in Munnar and check in to your comfortable hotel.
Relax, enjoy a delicious dinner, and unwind in the
peaceful mountain atmosphere.
Day 04
Breakfast & Dinner Included 
Stay: Hotel in Munnar 
Explore the Beauty of Munnar
Wake up to a refreshing morning and enjoy a delicious
breakfast at the hotel.
Today, explore the scenic highlights of Munnar, including
the peaceful Mattupetty Dam, the magical Echo Point,
and the picturesque Kundala Dam surrounded by lush
mountains. Visit the famous Eravikulam National Park
(subject to government guidelines), home to
breathtaking views and rare wildlife.
Also explore the vibrant Rose Garden and enjoy some
free time for local shopping and café hopping.
Return to the hotel, relax, and soak in the sirenic
mountain vibes.
Day 05
Breakfast & Dinner Included 
Stay: Hotel stay in Thekkady 
Start your day with breakfast and check out by 8:00 AM, then
enjoy a scenic drive to Thekkady, home to Kerala’s famous
wildlife sanctuary.
In the afternoon, you can experience the peaceful Periyar
Lake cruise (optional, direct payment), where you may spot
elephants, deer, wild boar, and other wildlife in their natural
habitat. You can also enjoy an elephant interaction
experience at Periyar.
In the evening, witness Kerala’s rich traditions with the
mesmerizing Kalaripayattu martial arts and Kathakali
cultural show.
Check in to your hotel, enjoy dinner, and relax.

Thekkady to Alleppey – Backwater

Day 06
After breakfast, check out and proceed to Alleppey,
known as the “Venice of the East.”
Experience the beauty of Kerala’s famous backwaters
with an optional boat ride (direct payment). Cruise
through peaceful canals surrounded by coconut trees,
villages, and scenic landscapes — truly a once-in-a-
lifetime experience.
Check in and relax for the evening. Enjoy a delicious
dinner and unwind.
  
Day 07
Alleppey to Cochin 
After breakfast, check out from your houseboat or hotel and
begin a scenic drive back to Kochi.
Upon arrival, explore the charming streets of Fort Kochi,
admire the iconic Chinese Fishing Nets, and enjoy some free
time for local shopping if time permits.
Later, check in to your hotel and spend a relaxing evening
before your onward journey.
Staying in Kochi tonight ensures a comfortable and stress-free
end to your Kerala adventure, avoiding early morning travel
before your train journey.
Breakfast Included 
Stay: Hotel in Cochin
Day 08
Cochin to Ahmedabad
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and prepare for
your journey back home. 
Proceed to Ernakulam Railway Station to board your train
to Ahmedabad, taking along unforgettable memories of
your Kerala adventure. We hope you had an amazing
experience and look forward to welcoming you on another
memorable journey with us! 
Breakfast Included
Please note that transportation from the railway station to
the hotel is not included and should be arranged by the
participants. Dinner on this day is also self-paid, giving you
the opportunity to explore the local cuisine at your
convenience.
Day 09
Arrival in Ahmedabad
After an unforgettable journey through God's Own Country, arrive
back in Ahmedabad with countless memories, new friendships,
and amazing experiences to cherish forever. Until the next
adventure!
